Privacy policy

Last updated 8 August 2026 · Written to be read, not skimmed past.

01

What we collect

Your name and email from Google sign-in, your country for pricing, the positions and trades you record, and — only if you connect the bot — your Telegram chat id.

We do not collect card numbers (the payment provider handles those), broker credentials, or precise location.

02

How it is used

To run your account, send the alerts you asked for, bill the right amount in the right currency, and keep the service working. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

03

Where it lives

Data is stored with our hosting provider in the EU, encrypted in transit and at rest. Payment details live with Stripe or Razorpay under their own policies.

04

Your rights

Export everything from the Your data page at any time; deletion from the same page removes your account and data within 30 days, with backups aging out inside 90.

You can also email privacy@sutralgo.com to exercise any right UK GDPR gives you.

05

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in and one remembers your theme. There is no advertising or cross-site tracking.

We answer within two working days. If the answer changes a document, the change lands here with a fresh date.
