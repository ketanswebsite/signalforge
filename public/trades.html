What these mean

Sharpe ratio: return for the risk taken. Above 1.0 is considered good.

Max drawdown: the deepest fall from a high. Smaller is better.

Profit factor: money made divided by money lost. Above 1.5 points to a working rule.

Expectancy: what one average trade is worth. Positive means the rule earns its keep.

Annualised return: the pace of returns stretched over a year.

Streaks: runs of wins or losses in a row — a feel for how bumpy the ride has been.
